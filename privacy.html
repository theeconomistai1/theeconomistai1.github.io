Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

shorts-factory-app ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's videos and set their title, description, audience setting and scheduled publish time.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): only to read the signed-in channel's ID and name, so the app can refuse to upload to the wrong channel.

The app does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, viewing history or any other account data.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token and the channel's ID and name are saved only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by the app, because there is none.

Sharing

The app sends data only to Google, through the YouTube Data API, to carry out the uploads. It does not sell, share, or transfer data to anyone else, and it uses no advertising or analytics services.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's stored access.
